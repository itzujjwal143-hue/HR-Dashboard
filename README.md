# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, performance, salary hikes, and other HR-related metrics.

The dashboard consists of **two pages**: an HR Overview page and an Attrition Analysis page. It helps HR teams understand workforce trends, identify employee groups with higher attrition, and explore factors associated with employees leaving the organization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key HR metrics and workforce statistics.
- Analyze employee attrition across job roles and departments.
- Understand attrition patterns based on age, experience, and tenure.
- Explore relationships between attrition, overtime, and promotion history.
- Compare employee performance and salary hikes across job roles and education fields.
- Support data-driven HR analysis and retention planning.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. HR Overview

Provides a high-level overview of the organization's workforce and attrition.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Employees
- Total Attrition
- Average Age
- Attrition Rate
- Active Employees

**Visualizations:**
- Performance Rating by Education Field
- Average Salary Hike by Job Role
- Attrition Count by Job Role
- Attrition Count by Department
- Attrition Count by Education Field
- Attrition Count by Age Band and Gender
- Job Satisfaction by Job Role

### 2. Attrition Analysis

Focuses on employee attrition patterns across experience, tenure, promotion history, management, and overtime.

**Visualizations:**
- Attrition Count by Total Working Years
- Attrition Count by Years at Company
- Attrition Count by Current Manager and Department
- Attrition Count by Years Since Last Promotion
- Attrition Count by Overtime and Department

These visualizations help identify employee segments with higher attrition and explore workforce patterns that may be associated with employee turnover.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Modeling** — Organizing data for analysis

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard can help HR teams investigate questions such as:

- Which job roles and departments have higher employee attrition?
- Which age groups and genders show different attrition patterns?
- How does attrition vary with total working experience and years at the company?
- Is attrition associated with overtime across departments?
- How does attrition vary with time since the last promotion?
- How do performance ratings and salary hikes differ across employee groups?
- How does job satisfaction vary by job role?

*Actual findings should be added after analyzing the dashboard results. Relationships shown in the data do not necessarily establish causation.*

## 💡 Business Value

- Supports workforce monitoring and HR reporting.
- Helps identify employee groups that may need retention-focused analysis.
- Enables comparisons across departments, job roles, and employee demographics.
- Provides a consolidated view of important HR metrics.
- Supports more informed workforce planning and employee engagement initiatives.
